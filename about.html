Who we are

Discover the Kenyan coast with confidence.

CoastLink Travel is a coastal travel company helping visitors discover some of the most beautiful destinations along the Kenyan coast.

From comfortable transfers and coastal accommodation to unforgettable excursions, our goal is to make travelling easier, more comfortable and more enjoyable.

Whether you're visiting Mombasa, Malindi, Watamu, Kilifi or other coastal destinations, our team is ready to help you plan your journey.
